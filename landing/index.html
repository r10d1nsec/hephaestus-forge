How it works

Five phases from sentence to spec.

A guided wizard turns one line of intent into documents a build agent can act on.

01

Describe

Start with a sentence or a short paragraph. That's all Hephaestus needs to begin.

02

Interview

It asks the sharp questions — scope, users, constraints — and fills the gaps you'd have missed.

03

Blueprint

Get a recommended solution type, realistic scale, and an honest time estimate before you commit.

04

Specify

A full PRD and technical spec are forged — structured, opinionated, and build-ready.

05

Ship

Export a ZIP and hand it straight to your build agent, or keep iterating with Regenerate.
